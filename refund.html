Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

RTOBoss provides a digital record formatting and utility service. No physical goods are shipped. This policy explains when a customer may request cancellation or a refund for a digital service order.

1. Eligible refund situations

A customer may contact RTOBoss for a refund review in circumstances such as:

  • A payment was successfully debited more than once for the same order.
  • The payment was successfully captured but the order was not created or could not be processed because of a technical error on our side.
  • The order was accepted and paid for, but RTOBoss is unable to deliver the purchased digital service because of a technical or service-side failure.
  • A transaction is identified as an unauthorized or incorrectly processed payment and sufficient transaction information is provided for investigation.

2. Situations generally not eligible for refund

  • The customer entered an incorrect registration number, licence number, date of birth or other required information and the service was successfully processed for the submitted information.
  • The digital service was successfully delivered and the customer later changes their mind.
  • The customer has already received a correctly generated digital result for the order.
  • The requested reference is outside the supported data source, where this limitation was reasonably clear before purchase.
  • The customer expects the RTOBoss PDF to function as a government-issued document. RTOBoss does not issue government documents.

3. Cancellation before completion

Because the service is digital and processing may begin immediately after payment, cancellation may not be possible after the order has entered processing. A cancellation request should be made as early as possible by contacting support with the order ID and transaction details.

4. How to request a refund

Send your refund request to rtobosska@gmail.com and include:

  • Order ID.
  • Customer name and contact number/email used for the order.
  • Date and approximate time of payment.
  • Amount paid.
  • Payment transaction/reference ID, where available.
  • A short explanation of the issue.

For security, do not send your UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, OTP, banking password or full card number by email or phone.

5. Refund review

RTOBoss will review the order and payment information available to us and may request reasonable supporting information. Approval of a refund depends on the circumstances described in this policy and the result of the transaction review.

6. Refund method and processing time

Where a refund is approved, it will generally be initiated through the original payment method or the payment provider's supported refund mechanism. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account can depend on the payment gateway, issuing bank and other financial institutions.

RTOBoss will communicate the refund action through the contact details available for the order when reasonably possible.

7. Failed or pending payments

If money appears to have been debited but the RTOBoss order remains pending or failed, please contact support rather than making repeated payments immediately. We will check the order/payment status and take the appropriate action.
