RTOBoss is an independent, private digital record formatting and utility service. RTOBoss is not a government website and is not affiliated with, authorized by, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Government of India, MoRTH, Parivahan Sewa, any State Transport Department or any RTO.

Who We Are

RTOBoss is a digital service created to make certain vehicle and driving-licence record information easier to organize, read and keep as a digital reference. Our focus is on a simple online experience: enter the supported reference information, review the available result, complete the applicable order process, and receive a formatted digital PDF when the requested record is available.

We operate as an independent private service and do not claim to be a government department, government portal, RTO office or government document issuer.

What We Provide

Vehicle Registration Record Formatting

Where a supported vehicle registration reference is available in our active data source, RTOBoss can present the information in a structured digital format and generate a PDF for personal or business utility.

Indicative website pricing: ₹100 for standard public two-wheeler/other eligible basic lookups, with higher prices shown for selected vehicle categories where applicable.

Driving Licence Record Formatting

Where a supported driving licence reference is available in our active data source, RTOBoss can present the available details in a structured digital format and generate a PDF for personal or business reference.

Indicative website pricing: ₹100 for public DL lookup/service, unless a different price is clearly displayed at checkout.

Partner / Agent Services

RTOBoss may provide partner or agent access for eligible users who need repeated document-lookup and formatting utility. Agent pricing, onboarding requirements and account terms are shown within the relevant part of the website.

How the Service Works

  • Select the type of digital record you need.
  • Enter the required reference information.
  • Review the information available for the requested reference.
  • Proceed through the displayed order and payment flow when a paid service applies.
  • After successful processing, access the formatted digital PDF through the website.

Important Service Limitation

RTOBoss only returns records available through its current data source and service configuration. A government record may exist even when RTOBoss does not have a corresponding result available. RTOBoss does not guarantee that every vehicle or licence number will return a result.

The PDFs generated by RTOBoss are formatted digital copies for personal or business utility. They are not government-issued documents and should not be represented as such.

Our Approach

We aim to provide clear pricing, transparent service information, accessible customer support and a straightforward digital delivery process. We also aim to keep payment, privacy, terms and refund information clearly available to customers before they place an order.

Payment Processing
